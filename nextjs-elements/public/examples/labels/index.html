Press sheet labels

Every job’s approved press sheet is filed for the next run, labelled with its barcode, filing number and the colour standard the customer signed off on. Those labels were hand-written. The original tool found the order line, filled the label in and printed a batch on a label printer. This one prints to whatever printer you have, or to PDF.
