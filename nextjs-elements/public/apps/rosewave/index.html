Try it

See the app as
  • Swipe up or downNext or previous photo. The buttons stay put and only the name fades over.
  • Tap the photoThe controls slide away, so you see it as a background. Tap again to bring them back. A double tap opens the choice of home, lock or both.
  • The four squares, top rightThe gallery. Tap a set's banner to see its photos as tiles, then tap a tile to jump to it. "All photos" is a banner of its own.
  • The two lines, top leftGallery, prints, then Experience (rotation, blur, a folding phone’s inner screen, membership) and Settings (folding phone, your old wallpaper).
  • RotationOn lock, on a timer from seconds to days, or with the sun. You choose what it draws from: everything, your faves, any sets, or a mix.
  • BlurFor members. "Blur the wallpaper" in Experience shows your wallpaper full screen with a slider that has four stops, from off to heavy. Nothing changes until you press "Use this blur".
