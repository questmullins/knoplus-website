Knowledge base

Simple answers before the repair begins.

What kind of parts do you use?

Quality OEM parts or high-quality NAPA aftermarket parts, depending on the vehicle and repair.

When should I change my oil?

Many vehicles need oil service around every 3,000 miles or once a year, but the owner manual should guide the exact interval.

When should tires be changed or rotated?

Many tires last 30,000 to 40,000 miles. Rotation is commonly recommended around 7,000 to 10,000 miles.

When do I change a timing belt?

Many vehicles need timing belt service around 60,000 miles, while some run closer to 90,000 miles. Check the model-specific schedule.

Why is my A/C not working?

Common causes include leaks, malfunctioning parts, or low refrigerant. Proper A/C repair requires diagnostic equipment and recharge service.

What does a check-engine light mean?

It means the vehicle computer detected a fault. A flashing light is more urgent and should be inspected quickly.

How do I know if my brakes are bad?

Warning lights, squeaking, scratching, vibration, and longer stopping distance are all reasons to schedule a brake inspection.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes, but bringing outside parts can affect parts and labor warranty coverage. Shop-supplied quality parts are recommended.
