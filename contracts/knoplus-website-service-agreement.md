## 2. Project Scope

Knoplus provides website design, customization, development, launch support, and optional website care services for small and independent businesses.

The exact project scope, price, timeline, and deliverables will be confirmed in a written proposal, invoice, quote, or order form. If the proposal conflicts with this Agreement, the proposal controls the project-specific details while this Agreement controls the general terms.

## 3. Best Fit and Scope Boundaries

Knoplus specializes in pre-built website experiences, fast launch timelines, clear service pages, lead-generation flows, contact forms, and practical ongoing management.

Knoplus is not primarily an e-commerce, payment processing, marketplace, membership platform, or custom software provider. Projects requiring shopping carts, subscriptions, customer accounts, payment processing, complex checkout logic, large product catalogs, inventory systems, heavy custom modifications, or ongoing custom feature development may require a separate specialist, a separate quote, or may be declined.

If a requested feature falls outside Knoplus' normal service model, Knoplus will make reasonable efforts to identify the limitation before work begins or when the limitation becomes clear.

## 4. Website Build Options

### Template Website

A template website starts from a proven framework and is customized for the Client's business, branding, content, imagery, services, and goals. Template website work may include updates to colors, copy, images, service sections, contact details, layout emphasis, and light interface adjustments.

Template websites are intended to provide a professional launch path without the cost of a fully custom design system. Work outside the template structure may require a custom quote.

### Custom Website

A custom website is designed from the ground up with original layouts, branding direction, user experience, visual systems, integrations, or content structure. Custom websites are quoted separately based on the requested scope.

## 5. Included Launch Services

Unless otherwise stated in the proposal, a website build may include:

- Mobile-friendly design
- Contact form integration or contact workflow setup
- Cloudflare or static deployment support
- SSL configuration
- Client-owned domain connection
- Basic SEO setup
- Launch support

Basic SEO setup may include page titles, descriptions, sitemap support, index-friendly page structure, and practical keyword placement. Knoplus does not guarantee search ranking, traffic, leads, sales, or indexing speed.

## 7. Domain Ownership and Domain Setup

Client is responsible for purchasing, owning, maintaining, and renewing their own domain name unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

Knoplus may help the Client choose a registrar, purchase a domain, connect the domain to the website, configure DNS records, and support SSL setup. This assistance does not transfer ownership of the domain to Knoplus.

Domain registration fees, renewal fees, transfer fees, registrar fees, email service fees, and other third-party domain costs are separate from Knoplus website pricing and are paid directly by the Client unless otherwise stated in writing.

Knoplus does not guarantee that a desired domain name will be available, remain available, or be accepted by a registrar.

## 8. Website Care Plans

Website care plans are optional monthly services after launch.

### Self Managed

Self Managed is $0/month. Client receives the website after launch and manages future updates, hosting decisions, renewal obligations, technical follow-through, and content changes.

### Standard Care

Standard Care is $39/month and may include:

- Hosting management
- SSL monitoring
- Client-owned domain and DNS connection assistance
- Basic uptime checks
- Monthly plugin/static asset check where applicable
- Up to 30 minutes of content updates per month

### Business Care

Business Care is $99/month and may include:

- Everything in Standard Care
- Priority support
- Monthly performance and SEO health check
- Minor layout polish for seasonal offers or service changes
- Up to 1 hour of content updates per month

Monthly update time does not roll over unless stated in writing. Content updates are limited to reasonable text, image, service, contact detail, and light layout changes. New pages, redesigns, integrations, forms, booking systems, e-commerce, custom functionality, payment processing, customer accounts, and emergency repair work may require separate billing or may fall outside Knoplus' service model.

## 9. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to provide timely access, content, approvals, and information needed to complete the project, including:

- Logo files, brand assets, colors, and imagery
- Business information, services, locations, and contact details

- Review notes and approvals

Client is responsible for ensuring that submitted content, claims, images, trademarks, testimonials, policies, and business information are accurate, lawful, and authorized for use.

Reasonable revisions may be included as stated in the proposal. Revisions that change the approved direction, add scope, require new pages, require new functionality, or revisit completed work may require additional billing.

Knoplus will use reasonable care when providing launch support or website care services. However, no website can be guaranteed to be uninterrupted, error-free, immune from security incidents, or permanently compatible with all browsers, devices, third-party services, or platform changes.

Knoplus is not liable for issues caused by Client edits, third-party platforms, expired domains, failed payments, DNS changes made by others, unsupported plugins, compromised accounts, or services outside Knoplus control.

## 15. Cancellation and Termination

Either Party may end a monthly care plan with written notice. Website build cancellation terms, refunds, and remaining balances will be handled according to the proposal or invoice.

Work completed before cancellation may be billed and may not be refundable. Knoplus may pause or terminate services for nonpayment, abusive communication, unlawful requests, or requests that create unreasonable risk.

To the fullest extent allowed by law, Knoplus is not liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, punitive, or lost-profit damages. Knoplus total liability for a project is limited to the amount the Client paid to Knoplus for the specific service giving rise to the claim.
